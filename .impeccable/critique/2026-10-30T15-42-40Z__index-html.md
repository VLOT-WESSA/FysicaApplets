## Overall Impression

A distinctive, coherent lab-notebook world that already feels made for this school. The weak spot is not the look but the measuring loop: on the screens where students actually measure, feedback lands off-screen, phones lose the canvas, and keyboard/screen-reader users get unnamed sliders and stateless toggles. The single biggest opportunity: make every measuring applet follow one task spine (instellen, meten, verwerken) with the measurement card always in view.

## What's Working

- Coherent visual world: one --h variable drives every accent; ink frames and hard shadows read as a lab notebook without being childish.
- Physics-first drawing when overlays are on: interferentie lines and wavefronts, kernreactor's cause-effect caption and temperature scale.
- Real measurement tooling: slinger chronometer + table + "Kopieer tabel"; videoanalyse 4-step stepper with calibration.
