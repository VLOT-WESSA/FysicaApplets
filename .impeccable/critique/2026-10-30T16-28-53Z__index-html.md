## Overall Impression

Trustworthy and coherent; the physics and the measurement loop are now correct. What holds it back: canvas text still shrinks below legibility on laptops, beamers and phones, toggle state is announced late or only by colour, and the shared side-panel system is defined but not used.

## What's Working
